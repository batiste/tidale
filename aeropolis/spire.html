Spire

Aeropolis hand-management variant on a hex tower. Every card is an action or coin.

Playtest Undercity

Each player has two pawns on the same tower: a Citizen at the top, an Outcast at the base. Play cards for their action, or cash them as coin to move a pawn and buy. Where a pawn stops, its hex activates. When your two pawns meet, the game ends: you score the height of your lower pawn.

Setup

  1. Board: the tower (see The Tower).
  2. Each player: Citizen on a top hex, Outcast on a base hex (spread out), the starter cards in hand, coin.
  3. Shuffle the market deck and reveal cards.

Turn

Clockwise, take one:

  • Action: play 1 card and resolve its text.
  • Move: step one of your pawns hex by hex, paying each hex's cost (see Coin).
  • Regroup: only, and always, when your hand is empty. Take back your used cards; then your burned cards become used. Some hexes and cards also make you Regroup.

Played cards are used: face up in front of you until you Regroup.

Coin

  • Coin is the only currency: it pays for steps and purchases. Keep unspent coin between turns.
  • Cash: whenever you pay, you may discard cards from your hand as coin, worth their value. They are used.
  • Burn: a cashed card may be burned for double value. It skips your next Regroup.
  • Moving down is not free: your Citizen earns coin by falling only through cards such as Sell Out.
  • A hex holds 1 pawn, except in the top zone (dashed). Top-zone pawns cannot push, be pushed or swap.
  • Any move (including card actions) activates the hex where the pawn stops.
  • Buy: pay the card's cost in coin. It goes to your hand.

Climb and Push

  • Climb 1: move your pawn to a free adjacent hex in the row above.
  • Push 1 step down: you move the rival pawn to a free adjacent hex in the row below.
  • Climb, push and swap are not moves: no activation. No free hex: nothing happens.

The Tower

rows of hexes, top to base. Rows of equal width are staggered by half a hex. Height counts from the base (1) to the top (). No named hex in the top zone or on the base row.

End

  • When a player's Outcast is level with or above their Citizen, every other player takes 1 last turn, then the game ends.
  • Score: the height of your lower pawn. Tie: higher Citizen.

Playtest Questions

  • Game length: how many turns before someone meets? Tune the row widths, costs and fall income.
  • Burn: is skipping one Regroup the right price for double coin?
  • Regroup hexes: too strong, since they return the cards just played to reach them?
  • Sell Out: is 2 rows for 2 coin enough for the Citizen to come down in the first half?
  • Push (market only): fun interaction or too punishing?
  • Are there enough reasons for the Citizen to come down (buy, scrap, recall)?
