Aeropolis — Prototype Cards

Two characters and an example seven-card starter deck. Each player begins with 2 coins. A deck card reads two ways: played into a character line, or kept in hand until the discard phase. Every played card has a cube socket. The face-down Intrigue mode is Print at true 57 × 89 mm with ⌘P.

Characters One of each per player, in play from setup. They anchor the left end of their line.

    Example Starter Deck — 7 cards Identical for every player in this draft. Card procedures and terminology are defined in the draft rules.

      Noodle Shop — Coin-cost samples Bought from the shared Noodle Shop for coin before joining your hand. After purchase, each card follows the same process as the starter deck. Costs and effects are first-pass examples. Revolutionary Organizer and Echo are playtest probes for the Other Line rules, priced above the rest of the shop since their effect scales with board state instead of paying a flat amount.

        Conflict Cards One revealed at the start of each round from a separate Conflict-card deck. Not played from hand and not part of a line — it sets that round's Reward and Condition for the conflict, plus the Refill Bonus added to Replenish (2 × player count + this card's bonus).

          Revolution Conflict Cards Replaces the Conflict pile once the Revolution begins — 3 of these shuffled in instead. Same shape as a Conflict card, raised stakes.
