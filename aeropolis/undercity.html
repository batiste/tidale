Undercity

Aeropolis hand-building variant. No rounds: when your hand is empty, you spend a turn to regroup.

Playtest Aeropolis

Play one card per turn to push your cubes around the city. When your hand is empty, you spend a turn to Regroup: score the districts you control and take your cards back. Buy new cards with your reputation, but every purchase brings your Citizen down toward your Outcast, and when they meet the game ends.

Setup

  1. Board: the 7 locations of Double Life (6 in a ring, Noodle Shop in the centre, adjacent to all except the Counting House and the Scrapyard). No cubes on it.
  2. Each player: 12 cubes (5 in reserve, 7 in stock), the 3 starter cards in hand, Citizen token on 12 (top of the Spire), Outcast token on 0.
  3. Shuffle the market deck and reveal 5 cards.

Turn

Clockwise, play 1 card from your hand and resolve it. Played cards stay face up in front of you. If your hand is empty at the start of your turn, your turn is Regroup instead.

Regroup

  1. Score: each Citizen district you control, Citizen +1; each Outcast district, Outcast +1.
  2. Buy: if you have a cube in the Noodle Shop, you may buy 1 market card (see Market).
  3. Take back: return all your played cards to your hand.
  4. Scrap: if you have a cube in the Scrapyard, you may remove 1 card in your hand from the game. You cannot go below 2 cards.
  5. Hire: if you have a cube in the Counting House, move 1 cube from your stock to your reserve.

A thin hand regroups, and scores, more often. Some market cards let you Regroup early (the card is your turn).

Control

You control a location if you have more cubes there than any other player; ties: nobody. Each location has one effect. Citizen districts (High Forum, Gaming Den) feed your Citizen, Outcast districts (Rust Bar, Cable Lift) feed your Outcast. The Noodle Shop, Scrapyard and Counting House give no Spire; a single cube there unlocks a Regroup step.

Starter Cards

Market

Pay a card's cost by moving your Citizen down that many spaces. You cannot pay more than the gap to your Outcast. The bought card goes into your hand; refill the market from the deck.

The Spire

  • Your Citizen never rises above 12. Your Outcast never passes your Citizen.
  • When your two tokens meet, every other player takes 1 last turn, then the game ends.
  • Score: your lower token. Tie: higher Citizen.

Playtest Questions

  • Game length: how many turns before someone's tokens meet? Tune with the Spire height (12).
  • Card costs vs how fast Citizen control repays them.
  • Regroup scoring: +1 per district, or more?
  • Buying now also slows your Regroup: should market costs drop by 1?
  • Scrapyard: is thinning worth fighting for?
  • Noodle Shop requirement for buying: interesting or annoying?
