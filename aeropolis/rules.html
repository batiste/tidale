Aeropolis

Draft rules for the current playtest version.

Concept Cards Board Playtest

Each player manages a Citizen and an Outcast. Play cards into their two lines, slot cubes from the city, and decide when to spend status for resources. This is an early draft: everything is subject to change.

Setup

  1. Each player get one Citizen character and one Outcast character cards (draft).
  2. Each player places their Citizen token at the top of the Spire and their Outcast token at the bottom.
  3. Give each player the current example starter deck of 7 cards.
  4. Give each player 2 coins.
  5. Create a deck of 6 random Conflict cards.
  6. Place 1 cube on each board location.

The character pool, player count, and exact starting setup are still playtest parameters.

Round Structure

  1. Draw: each player draws 4 cards from their deck.
  2. Action: in turn order, each player takes one turn — Play, Discard, or Intrigue (see Taking An Action) — until every player has discarded and has no committed card left to resolve.
  3. Replenish: add cubes equal to 2 × the number of players, plus this round's Conflict card's Refill Bonus (see Conflict). Starting at the Conflict card's named location, place them 1 at a time around the locations in board order, looping back and skipping any already at its cap (see Locations and Access), until every cube is placed. Empty every line: discard every card in it, face-up or face-down, and return their cubes to the supply.

Taking An Action

Each turn is one of three actions. Each player must take an action, until they have nothing to do.

Play

Play 1 card into a line (aligned left). Pay that position's play cost, set by the line's character, then Recruit 1 cube from an eligible location. Recruiting resolves that location's reward; depending on whether you played the card face up or face down, a second effect also resolves — the player chooses which of the two effects to resolve first:

  • Face up: Slot the recruit in the card's normal socket and resolve the socket's effect.
  • Face down (Intrigue): Sink that line's character 1 as a cost, and place the cube on the top of the card (see Conflict).

A card marked Broker may be played into either line. A Citizen card belongs in the Citizen line, and an Outcast card belongs in the Outcast line.

Discard

If you hold any cards, you may spend your turn discarding your remaining hand to resolve each card's Discard effect.

Intrigue

When you have no cards in hand, if you have committed face-down cards, spend 1 turn resolving one of them. Choose the rightmost face-down card in your line, flip it face up, and choose where to socket the cube: the card's normal socket, or the intrigue socket.

Locations and Access

Each location holds up to a set number of cubes — 2 for most locations, 3 for the Noodle Shop. A location is available only while it has at least 1 cube. Citizen locations are eligible when playing into the Citizen line. Outcast locations are eligible when playing into the Outcast line. Shared locations, including the Noodle Shop, are eligible for either line.

If no eligible location has a cube, you usually cannot play the card. Recruiting takes the cube from the location and resolves that location's reward; slotting places the recruit in the card's normal socket and resolves the socket's effect — the player chooses which of the two to resolve first.

Conflict

Each new round a new conflict is revealed and placed on the board. It sets that round's Reward, Condition, and Refill Bonus — a named board location where this round's Replenish starts, plus 0–2 extra cubes (see Round Structure — Replenish).

A player typically commits to the conflict with a face-down (see Taking An Action).

Once everybody has passed, it is time to resolve the conflict. A player's total for the conflict is the sum of the Intrigue values of their cards. The highest total wins and claims the reward. In case of a tie, the Citizen and the Outcast position are used as a tiebreaker, in this order. If still tied, the reward is discarded.

The Revolution

The Revolution begins immediately when one of those 2 conditions is met:

  • A player's Citizen and Outcast meet on the Spire: this players raises his Citizen and Outcast by 2.
  • The Conflict deck is exhausted.

Replace the Conflict pile with a pile of 3 random Revolution Conflict cards (playtest: replace the current conflict) — stronger versions of the normal Conflict cards, same Reward/Condition/Refill Bonus shape (see Conflict).

Glossary

Recruit

Take 1 grey cube (a recruit) from an eligible location and place it in your card's socket. You then can take the location's effect, and card's socket effect, in either order.

Plant

Take a cube from the supply, and place it in any empty slot in this line: another card's socket, or the character anchoring it. Some cards name a different line to plant into instead — follow the card's text, shown with its own icon (see Other Line).

Rise

Move the named character 1 space upward the Spire track.

Sink

Move the named character 1 space downward the Spire track.

Other Line

The line opposite the one a card is played into: the Outcast line for a Citizen card, the Citizen line for an Outcast card. For a Broker card, whichever line it was not played into.

Refresh

Discard 1 card on offer in the Noodle Shop and replace it with a new one from the market deck.

End Of The Game

The game ends after 3 Revolution rounds have been resolved. Players score Victory Points from their lowest position on the Spire, plus any other scoring effects.

If a player manage to have their Citizen and Outcast meet at the top of the Spire, they win immediately and the game is over.
