Double Life

Working title. A lighter spin-off of Aeropolis: shared actions, one shared deck, one city.

Aeropolis

Each player runs a Citizen and an Outcast. Draft actions from one shared row to place cubes in the city. Control a location to climb the Spire with the side it feeds. You score only your weaker side, so neither life can be neglected.

Components

  • Board: 7 locations and the Spire track (0–30).
  • Residents: 30 grey cubes, the city's population.
  • Cubes: 12 per player, in the player's colour.
  • Spire tokens: 2 per player, one Citizen, one Outcast.
  • Action deck: 15 cards (see Actions).

Setup

  1. Place 3 residents on each location, 4 on the Noodle Shop.
  2. Place both Spire tokens of each player on 0.
  3. Each player, starting with the last player and going counter-clockwise, takes a free Recruit action.

Board

Six locations form a ring; the Noodle Shop sits in the centre, adjacent to all. Each location shows what its controller gains at cleanup.

Round Structure

  1. Reveal: shuffle the action deck and reveal 4 actions face up.
  2. Actions: in turn order, take 1 face-up action, resolve it immediately, then refill the row from the deck. Continue until each player has taken 3 actions.
  3. Cleanup: see below.

A taken action you cannot resolve is wasted; you may still take it to deny it.

Actions

ActionCopiesEffect
Recruit5At one location, return up to 2 residents to the supply. Place 1 of your cubes there for each.
Move4Move up to 2 of your cubes 1 step each.
Agitate3At a location where you have a cube, push 1 opponent cube to an adjacent location.
Seize3At a location where you have more cubes than an opponent, return 1 of their cubes to their reserve.

Control

You control a location if you have more cubes there than any other player. Ties: nobody controls it.

Cleanup

  1. Score: each location's controller advances their Spire tokens as shown on the location.
  2. Repopulate: refill every location to its starting residents.
  3. Reset: discard the row, shuffle all actions into the deck, pass first player left.

End Of The Game

The game ends after cleanup of round 5. Your score is your lower Spire token. Tie: higher other token wins; then shared victory.

Playtest Questions

  • Core test: at 4 players, do people repeatedly want the same face-up action?
  • Row size (4) and actions per player (3): enough tension, enough agency?
  • Deck mix: is Seize too strong, is Recruit too common?
  • Round count (5) and Spire length (30).
