Spire

Rules in short

  • Districts are High City (2–4 shares ◆) or Low City (1–3 shares).
  • Betting cards: everyone holds 1, 2, 3. Every played card goes to your discard; when your hand is empty, take all your cards back.
  • Each turn, one district is auctioned. Everyone plays a betting card face down (or passes), then all reveal. The highest card wins; between equal cards, the player closest to the auctioneer's left wins.
  • Winner: takes the district, their Citizen sinks by their card. Players who bid and lost: Outcast rises 1. Nobody bids: the district burns, every Outcast rises 1.
  • Revolution: when a player's Outcast reaches their Citizen, the current round is finished, then the game ends. Also ends when the deck runs out.
  • Score: High City shares × your Citizen's tier + Low City shares × your Outcast's tier. Tiers: heights 1–7 ×1, 8–14 ×2, 15–20 ×3. Bidding sinks your Citizen (your High City is worth less); losing raises your Outcast (your Low City is worth more).
