3–5 players.

## Concept

A city on the brink of revolution, torn between 5 factions.
Each player drafts 2 characters: a **Citizen** (High City) and an **Outcast** (Low City).
Each character flies a public **banner** (faction); each player also holds a hidden **allegiance**.
Players spread influence, flip the city's banners, and time the Revolution.

---

## Terms

- **Faction**: Crown, Church, Guilds, Commune, Underworld.
- **Banner**: the faction shown on a character or a location.
- **Allegiance**: a player's hidden faction card.
- **Influence**: a player's cube on a location.
- **Spire**: the shared track, heights 1–20. It is both the clock and the score.
- **Control**: most influence at a location. Tie: nobody.

Resources: gold and arms only.

---

## Components

- Board: the Spire (1–20) and the city: 4 High City locations above 4 Low City locations.
- 5 banner tokens per faction (location banners).
- 10 characters: 5 Citizens, 5 Outcasts, each with a banner and a unique ability.
- 5 allegiance cards (one per faction).
- Per player: 2 pawns (Citizen, Outcast), influence cubes.

---

## Setup

- Draft characters: each player ends with 1 Citizen and 1 Outcast with different banners.
- Deal 1 allegiance card face down to each player (any faction, possibly one of their banners).
- Each location gets a random banner.
- Citizen pawns at height 20, Outcast pawns at height 1.

---

## The Spire (clock and score)

- Every action costs **time**: 1–3 steps. Acting with the Citizen moves it **down**; acting with the Outcast moves it **up**.
- **Turn order**: the player whose two pawns are furthest apart plays next (they have spent the least time). Tie: the one who acted least recently.
- **Score**: the heights of your two pawns, added. The Outcast climbing gains points; the Citizen descending loses them. So Citizen actions must be stronger per step: they are the expensive, powerful ones.
- **Payout lines** at heights 5, 10 and 15: when any pawn crosses one, every location pays its controller (once per line per pawn type).

---

## Turn

Choose one of your characters:

1. Move it to a location in its own city (High for the Citizen, Low for the Outcast). One character per location.
2. Place 1 influence there and take the location's action.
3. Move it on the Spire by the action's time cost.

**In tandem**: if your other character is directly above or below, also take the bonus printed between the two locations.

---

## Locations

Each location: an action (with its time cost), a control reward (at payout lines), a banner.
Examples (to be designed):

| High City  | Low City   | Tandem bonus  |
|------------|------------|---------------|
| Palace     | Sewers     | flip a banner |
| Cathedral  | Catacombs  | +1 influence  |
| Exchange   | Docks      | +2 gold       |
| Barracks   | Slums      | +1 arm        |

**Flip a banner** (expensive): an action at a location you control, costing 3 time and 2 gold: replace its banner with one of your factions (banner or allegiance).

---

## Revolution

Triggered when any player's Citizen and Outcast **meet** on the Spire.

1. Every location resolves an uprising: its controller scores bonus points if one of their factions (banners or allegiance) matches the location's banner.
2. The rules change for the rest of the game (to choose, see Open questions), e.g. the High/Low barrier falls (characters go anywhere), or banners lock.
3. Play continues until a second pair meets, or all pawns pass the meeting point.

---

## End and scoring

- Spire heights of both pawns.
- Banner scoring: each location pays its influence holders whose factions match its banner: 1 point per influence for a public banner, 2 for your allegiance.
- Allegiance guess: each player names one opponent's allegiance; a correct guess steals 3 points.

- End: how long after the Revolution? A second meeting, or a fixed number of turns?
- Allegiance: may it duplicate a public banner (a readable all-in bet), or must it differ?
