Banners: Simple

Aeropolis, a stripped-down Banners. Two characters, two banners, one score: your weaker banner. 3–5 players.

Your Citizen descends the Spire, your Outcast climbs it, and each flies a banner. Spread influence, take control, raise your banners. You score only your weaker banner: neglect one character and you lose.

Components

  • Board: the Spire (heights 1 to ) and the city: 4 High City locations above 4 Low City locations, in 4 columns.
  • Banners of the 5 factions, enough to hang one at every location.
  • Per player: 2 pawns (Citizen, Outcast), influence cubes.

Setup

  1. Deal each player two different factions: their Citizen's banner and their Outcast's banner. No two players may share both.
  2. Hang a random banner at each location.
  3. Citizens on Spire , Outcasts on 1. Pawns start off the city.

Turn

The player who has spent the least time plays (time = how far your Citizen has sunk + how far your Outcast has risen; tie: whoever acted least recently).

  1. Move one character to another location of its city: the Citizen in the High City, the Outcast in the Low City. Pawns may share a location.
  2. Act there, one of:
    • Place influence: the Citizen places , the Outcast .
    • Raise one of your banners, if you control the location (most influence; tie: nobody).
  3. Tandem: if your other character stands in the same column, it may act at its location too.
  4. Spend time: the Citizen sinks , the Outcast rises .

City

End and score

  • When your Outcast is level with or above your Citizen, they meet: you score now and are out. The game ends when every player is out.
  • Score: each of your banners totals, over the locations flying it, 1 per influence you have there, + where you have control. Your score is your lower total. Tie: the higher other total, then most influence in the city.
