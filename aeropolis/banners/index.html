Banners

Aeropolis, draft 1. Five factions, two characters per player, 3–5 players. Cards: cards.html. Simplified version: Banners: Simple.

You play a Citizen descending from the top of the Spire and an Outcast climbing from its base. Every action costs time: it moves the pawn that acted. Each character flies a banner. Spread influence and hang your banners over it. When a player's two characters meet, the Revolution ends the game.

Factions

Components

  • Board: the Spire (heights 1 to ) and the city: 4 High City locations above 4 Low City locations.
  • Banners: per faction.
  • Characters: 5 Citizens and 5 Outcasts, one of each per faction.
  • Per player: 2 pawns (Citizen, Outcast), influence cubes.

Setup

  1. Deal each player 1 Citizen and 1 Outcast. Redeal if a player's two banners match, or if two players share both. Your banners are your two characters' factions.
  2. Shuffle the banners and hang 1 at each location. The rest form the reserve, face up.
  3. Citizen pawns on Spire , Outcast pawns on 1. Character pawns start off the city.
  4. Choose a first player.

Spire

  • Time: every action has a cost (⧗). The pawn that acted moves that many heights: the Citizen sinks, the Outcast rises. You cannot take an action that would move a pawn off the Spire.
  • Turn order: the player who has spent the least time plays next. Time spent = how far your Citizen has sunk + how far your Outcast has risen. Tie: whoever acted least recently; at the start, seating order from the first player.

Turn

  1. Choose one of your characters. Move it to another location of its own city (several pawns may share a location).
  2. Place 1 influence there. Then you may take the location's action.
  3. Tandem: if your other character stands in the same column, you may also take that column's tandem bonus.
  4. Move the pawn on the Spire by the action's cost, even if you skipped the action.

Control: most influence at a location. Tie: nobody. Removed influence goes back to its owner. With an empty supply, placing does nothing.

Replace a banner: swap it with any banner from the reserve.

City

Revolution

  • At the end of a turn, if your Outcast is level with or above your Citizen, they meet: you score now and are out (no more turns, your score is final). When every player is out: the Revolution, and the game ends.
  • Score: each of your influence under one of your banners scores . Each location you control scores , whatever its banner. Tie: most influence in the city.
