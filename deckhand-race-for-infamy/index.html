A fast-paced engine-building game distilled into 108 cards.

Set sail in this compact card game that plays out over 5 quick rounds: draft cards to shape your strategy, then build your Fleet to gain momentum.

Love games where every decision powers you up? This fast-paced pirate adventure delivers that thrill with just a deck of cards and a quick setup. You'll be ready to play in minutes.

Balance growing your engine with taking risks to claim powerful Infamy cards. With simultaneous turns, shared objectives, and tough decisions at every step, there’s barely time to shout “Arrr!”
